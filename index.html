About

Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at KAUST, working under Prof. Helmut Pottmann on architectural geometry.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) in Thuwal, Saudi Arabia, originally from Ecuador. My interests span discrete differential geometry, sphere geometries, computational geometry, geometry processing, architectural geometry and computer graphics. I began as a theoretical physicist at Yachay Tech University, where a growing passion for geometry led me to discrete differential geometry and its applications. My research now centers on building computational pipelines that turn abstract geometric theory into practical tools for architectural design. I approach problems by first finding the right abstraction: exposing the underlying mathematical structure, then building efficient solutions. I work primarily in Python, with additional experience in C++ and Wolfram Mathematica, foundational work in GPU rendering and parallel computing with CUDA and Vulkan, and a working familiarity with functional programming in Haskell. I co-authored a paper that received the Best Technical Paper Award at SIGGRAPH Asia 2023, and I was named to the KAUST Dean's List in 2023 and 2024.
